    About MongArchive

    MongArchive.org is an independent archive and research platform created to preserve, organize, and share documented materials related to Mong history, identity, language, books, articles, archives, maps, research sources, and related topics.

    The purpose of MongArchive is to provide a respectful and organized place where materials can be preserved, reviewed, and made easier for future readers to find.

    MongArchive does not claim official institutional authority. Its credibility depends on careful organization, source review, responsible citations, correction review, and transparency about how materials are preserved and presented.

    Materials on MongArchive are provided for educational, research, and public reference purposes. Readers should verify important claims through credible sources, original records, and published scholarship when possible.
